# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Homeowners and household operators managing one or more properties use this app on a phone while standing at a panel, walking room-to-room, planning upgrades, or answering "what is on this circuit?" in the moment. Secondary users include electricians, family members, and future maintainers who need fast confidence in panel topology, room mappings, device notes, and live energy behavior.

## Product Purpose
Electrical Config is a field-ready home electrical map and operating console. It combines structured NocoDB inventory data with live Home Assistant telemetry so users can understand where power flows, what each breaker serves, how solar and EV charging affect usage, and how to safely maintain accurate records from the UI or AI chat. Success looks like faster troubleshooting, fewer stale records, better energy awareness, and high trust that edits made in the app write back cleanly to the source of truth.

## Brand Personality
Calm, capable, trustworthy. The product should feel like a premium utility tool: precise without being clinical, confident without being flashy, and approachable for a homeowner who is not an electrician. The tone should reward curiosity, reduce anxiety during maintenance tasks, and make live power and charging data feel legible rather than overwhelming.

## Anti-references
Avoid generic smart-home dashboard clutter, neon gamer energy panels, light-theme enterprise CRUD styling, and widget-heavy consumer energy apps that bury the important state. Avoid decorative motion, toy-like skeuomorphism, or dense alarm-red treatments that make normal power usage feel hazardous. Avoid chat UIs that feel disconnected from the authoritative home data model.

## Design Principles
- Put the operational answer first: surface the one fact a user needs right now before secondary detail.
- Make live energy legible at a glance: production, consumption, charging, and breaker load should read in seconds.
- Preserve field confidence: panel, room, and circuit interactions must feel safe, explicit, and reversible.
- Keep one visual language across browse, monitor, and edit modes so the tool remains trustworthy.
- Use AI as a fast control surface, not a mystery box: always preview intent, confirm scope, and report write-back results clearly.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Target WCAG 2.2 AA contrast and focus visibility across the dark theme. Respect reduced-motion preferences by simplifying animated energy flows and live pulsing states. Maintain 40x40px minimum touch targets for all mobile interactions, especially tabs, chips, circuit cards, and edit affordances. Use tabular numbers for live metrics, plain-language labels for electrical jargon where possible, and avoid color-only encoding for load severity, charging state, or write-back success or error states.
