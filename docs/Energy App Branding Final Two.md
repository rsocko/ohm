 
## Context  
  
AI-powered home electrical configuration + energy monitoring PWA. SvelteKit, mobile-first, dark UI, information-dense. Integrates NocoDB (circuit topology), Home Assistant (Emporia Vue + Enphase Solar), and natural language AI queries.  
  
Target user: Power user managing home electrical systems across multiple properties. Needs instant answers ("which circuit is the kitchen island on?") and live energy visibility.  
  
---  
  
## Finalist 1: Ohm  
  
### The Name  
  
**Ohm** (Ω) — the SI unit of electrical resistance. Also a near-homophone of "home."  
  
### Why It Works  
  
- **Instant recognition** — anyone with basic electrical knowledge knows the word  
- **The Ω symbol IS the logo** — no design needed, it's already iconic  
- **Ohm ≈ Home** — subconscious association with domestic/residential use  
- **Voice-friendly** — "Ask Ohm which circuit the kitchen is on"  
- **Universal** — works in every language (SI units are global)  
- **Maximum brevity** — 3 letters, 1 syllable  
  
### Brand Personality  
  
**In a word:** Authority.  
  
**Archetype:** The quiet expert. A precision instrument that simply *knows*. Doesn't explain itself — just delivers.  
  
| Attribute | Expression |  
|-----------|------------|  
| Tone | Direct, assured, minimal |  
| Complexity | Technical but never jargony |  
| Humor | Dry, rare, earned |  
| Metaphor | Instrument, tool, reference |  
| Energy | Calm confidence |  
  
### Taglines  
  
| Tagline | Use case |  
|---------|----------|  
| "Know your home, circuit by circuit." | Hero / marketing |  
| "Total resistance to chaos." | Personality-forward |  
| "Ask Ohm." | Conversational / AI interaction |  
| "Every circuit. Every watt. One answer." | Feature-focused |  
| "The Ω of home energy." | Iconic / minimal |  
  
### Visual Identity  
  
#### Logo  
  
The **Ω** symbol rendered in a geometric mono-weight stroke. Clean, mathematical, no embellishment. Works at 16px favicon and 200px splash screen.  
  
Variations:  
- **Primary mark:** Standalone Ω in medium weight  
- **App icon:** Ω inside a rounded square (iOS/PWA home screen)  
- **Wordmark:** "ohm" in lowercase geometric sans, with Ω replacing the 'o'  
- **Monochrome:** Single-color Ω for all backgrounds  
  
#### Color System  
  
| Role | Color | Hex | Usage |  
|------|-------|-----|-------|  
| Primary | Warm Amber | `#F5A623` | Active power, energy flow, primary actions |  
| Secondary | Cyan | `#00D4AA` | Solar generation, positive/inflow |  
| Surface | Deep Charcoal | `#1A1A2E` | App background |  
| Surface elevated | Slate | `#252540` | Cards, panels, modals |  
| Text primary | Off-white | `#EEEEF0` | Body text |  
| Text muted | Gray | `#8888A0` | Labels, secondary info |  
| Alert | Soft Red | `#FF6B6B` | Overloads, breaker trips, warnings |  
| Success | Green | `#4ADE80` | Normal operation, confirmed |  
  
**Rationale:** Amber-forward palette — energy is warm, electric, alive. Cyan provides contrast for solar/generation data. The palette is functional: colors map to meaning, not decoration.  
  
#### Typography  
  
| Use | Font | Weight | Notes |  
|-----|------|--------|-------|  
| Logotype | Custom Ω glyph | Medium | Geometric, mono-weight |  
| Headings | Inter | SemiBold (600) | Clean, neutral, legible |  
| Body | Inter | Regular (400) | Proven at small sizes |  
| Data/values | JetBrains Mono | Regular (400) | Tabular nums, watt readouts |  
  
#### Motion Language  
  
- **Transitions:** 150ms ease-out. Quick, purposeful. Never bouncy.  
- **Data updates:** Numeric values crossfade (no counting animations)  
- **Circuit highlighting:** Subtle amber pulse when a circuit is active/selected  
- **Loading:** Single Ω pulsing gently (opacity 0.4 → 1.0)  
  
### Voice & Copy Examples  
  
**AI response style:**  
> "Kitchen island outlets → Panel A, Circuit 14. 20A breaker, currently drawing 847W."  
  
**Error state:**  
> "Lost connection to Home Assistant. Last reading: 2 min ago."  
  
**Empty state:**  
> "No circuits mapped yet. Connect your NocoDB instance to get started."  
  
**Notification:**

## Finalist 2: Ohmni  
  
### The Name  
  
**Ohmni** — a portmanteau of "Ohm" (Ω, resistance) + "Omni" (all, everything). Pronounced "OH-m-nee."  
  
### Why It Works  
  
- **Ohm is in the name** — electrical credibility baked in  
- **Omni = all-seeing** — communicates the AI/intelligence angle  
- **Unique & trademarkable** — no existing major brands  
- **Zero SEO competition** — you own page 1 immediately  
- **Ohm ≈ Home still resonates** — the phonetic connection survives  
- **AI-native** — sounds like an intelligent system, not just a tool  
- **Scales** — works as product name, company name, platform name  
  
### Brand Personality  
  
**In a word:** Intelligence.  
  
**Archetype:** The all-seeing advisor. Proactively understands your home — not just answering questions but surfacing insights before you ask.  
  
| Attribute | Expression |  
|-----------|------------|  
| Tone | Confident, helpful, anticipatory |  
| Complexity | Makes the complex feel simple |  
| Humor | Occasional, warm |  
| Metaphor | Vision, awareness, intelligence |  
| Energy | Alert, proactive |  
  
### Taglines  
  
| Tagline | Use case |  
|---------|----------|  
| "See everything. Understand every circuit." | Hero / marketing |  
| "Your home's electrical intelligence." | Positioning statement |  
| "Omniscient about your ohms." | Personality-forward |  
| "Every watt, everywhere, all at once." | Playful / feature-rich |  
| "Ask once. Know always." | AI interaction |  
  
### Visual Identity

Variations:

- **Wordmark:** "ohmni" in lowercase with Ω replacing the 'o', or the 'Ω' dot on the 'i'

## Decision Framework  
  
### Choose Ohm if you value:  
- Radical simplicity and iconic minimalism  
- The Ω symbol doing all the heavy lifting  
- "Instrument" brand energy — a tool that needs no introduction  
- Voice-first interaction ("Ask Ohm" is unbeatable)  
- You're OK investing more in visual identity to compensate for name generality  
  
### Choose Ohmni if you value:  
- Full ownership across all channels from day one  
- The AI/intelligence story being front-and-center  
- Room to grow into a platform or suite of products  
- Discoverability without marketing spend  
- A brand with built-in personality and voice
